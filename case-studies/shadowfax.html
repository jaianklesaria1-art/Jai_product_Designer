Being honest

What’s real

The interviews, personas, competitor review, both full designs and the Usability Hub test with its heatmaps and scores.

What isn’t measured

The redesign was never shipped into Shadowfax’s app, so there’s no retention or order number. The idea that single-drop booking pushed senders to competitors was my assumption, not a figure I measured. The design covers the happy path, and had no accessibility check.

What I’d do next

  • Test the finished screens with senders, not just the two variants
  • Add a step indicator to the five-step order
  • Design failed-payment and no-rider states
  • Check contrast, tap targets and screen readers

What I learned

Build two, then let the test argue. A won more screens, and B was still the better product. Designing two complete options gave the test something real to compare.
