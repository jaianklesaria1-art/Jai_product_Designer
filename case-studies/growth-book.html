The problem

5things the app had to do

Busy parents, scattered records

Parents juggle work and childcare. Sharing details with paediatricians, recording milestones and keeping memories all compete for the little time they have.

The question I started with

How can one app make a baby's health simple to manage, and keep the memories, within a busy parent's day?

What the app had to do

  1. Centralised informationOne place for a child's development, anytime.
  2. User-friendly designClear for every parent, whatever their tech skills.
  3. Complete trackingGrowth, development and vaccinations together.
  4. Health and nutrition supportFood tracker, health tips and consultations.
  5. Intellectual enrichmentContent that helps a child's development.
Why not just a survey?

Raising a child is different for every parent. So I ran ethnographic studies and in-depth interviews with new and expecting parents, to see how their idea of childcare differs.

Empathy map

5pains when starting solid food

Mothers starting their baby on solid food

What they need to do

Feed healthy, tasty home-made food, make sure the baby gets enough nutrition, and get timely guidance on recipes.

What they see

Lots of ads for packed baby food. Friends and family recommend home-made instead, and gut feeling agrees, even when it takes time and isn't tasty.

What they hear

Home-made food is safer. Family offers many options, which makes it more confusing. Lots of talk about food allergies.

Who we empathised with

New mothers of babies under three, feeding them solids for the first time. They play the main role in feeding and make the final call.

Pains

  1. Finding out the nutrition in a meal
  2. Finding recipes babies like
  3. Measuring portion sizes
  4. Time and energy spent cooking
  5. Worry about food allergies

Gains

Timely guidance on nutritious, tasty, easy, allergy-free home-made recipes that keep the baby healthy.

What they say

"I strongly believe home-made food is healthier for babies. Babies are very choosy about their food. Not sure if my baby is getting a balanced diet with the right quantity and nutrition. How much water should a baby drink daily?"

What they do

Most feed home-made recipes they trust, but keep asking their mothers and relatives about quantity, new recipes and nutrition.

What helped me: the Hook model

4steps in a habit loop

A habit parents come back to

From Nir Eyal's book Hooked: How to Build Habit-Forming Products. A tracker only works if parents open it every day, so I built that loop into the app.

Trigger

External

  • Meal and water reminders, community notifications
  • Email
  • A crying, hungry baby

Internal

  • Wanting to care for the baby well
  • Wanting to stay connected with other mothers

Action

  • Open the app and view a recipe
  • Cook and feed the baby
  • Upload photos, comment in the mothers' channel
  • Read parenting tips

Investment

  • Baby's health chart and records
  • A complete food log
  • Allergy tracker
  • Profile and recognition in the community

Reward

  • A healthy baby
  • Likes, comments and followers in the community
  • Knowing more about baby care
and back to the next trigger
